### bubble app
* -- A simple text based app to capture key decision moments of your life in micro-journal format called PMOT(personal moments of truth). Each PMOT will be timestamped, and will contain atleast 3 parts take-off, turbulence, touchdown (all text) and an optional part anchor(that can be text, picture, video, audio or anything else)
